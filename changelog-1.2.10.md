## v1.2.10 – Monster Mash jam build

- Race a stitched-together monster through real streets (Accra only at the moment), checkpoint to checkpoint, against a friend: quick match, private rooms with join codes, or solo practice.
- Monster abilities: Tentacles to grab poles and swing round corners, Slime to leave a slippery trail.
- Monsters can't reverse: steer hard when stuck to spin on the spot and turn around.
- Smoother online play: automatic recovery from dropped connections, with a Rejoin button.
- Graphics quality adjusts automatically on slower phones, and there's a Graphics button.
