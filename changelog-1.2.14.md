## v1.2.14 – Build your monster

- **Build your monster:** pick a body (Stitched Brute or Bone Rattler) and one part for each slot before every race. Your last build is remembered.
  - **Legs:** Werewolf Legs (fastest on straights, slides in corners) or Spider Legs (grips corners and rough ground, 25% slower).
  - **Arms:** Tentacles (grab a pole and swing round it) or Crab Claws (hit a rival from behind to steal a part).
  - **Head:** Slime Gland (drop a slippery trail) or Jack-o'-Lantern (a glowing line shows the way to your next checkpoint).
- **Lose parts, steal parts:** hit another monster, or crash into a building at 60 km/h or more, and a part can fall off. Drive through it to pick it up again. Lose a part and your monster gets lighter and a little faster, so a stripped-down monster can still win.
- **Part slots in the race HUD,** with a flash when you lose one, plus parts lost, picked up and stolen on the results screen.
- Fixed a case where a monster creeping along a wall couldn't turn around.
