## v1.2.20 – Race with friends

- **Rooms for up to 4 players.** The player who creates a room is the host and starts the race with the Start button once everyone in the lobby is ready (2 players minimum). Quick match works the same way.
- **Rematch your way.** After a race, tap Rematch to head back to the lobby while others keep reading their results. The host can start the next race without anyone still on the results, and anyone left behind can join the one after.
- **Real gametags** in the lobby and results instead of Player 1, Player 2.
- **Esc opens a menu** (or tap Menu in the race HUD): graphics quality, performance info, tips and help. Online races keep running while it's open.
- **First-time tips** teach the controls, abilities and what to do when stuck. In practice mode they show every race.
- **Phones start driving automatically** when the race begins.
- **Improved reconnecting.**
- The build screen now fits short landscape screens.
