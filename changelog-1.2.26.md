## v1.2.26 – Sound

- **Monster sound effects:** heavy stomping footsteps, growls, thumping crashes, bones snapping when a part falls off, and a sparkly chime when you grab one back.
- **Ability sounds:** a tentacle whip, a slime squelch and a claw snap, played the moment you press the button. You also get a "not available" thud if the ability isn't ready.
- **Race flow:** a drumbeat countdown, a roar at GO, and a victory roar when you finish.
- **Music:** a spooky lobby theme and a faster race theme.
- **Volume control:** a volume slider plus separate sound effects and music switches in the Esc menu.
