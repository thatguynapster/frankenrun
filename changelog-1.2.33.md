## Monster models, a new lobby and five routes

- **Real monster models:** your monster now stands on two legs. The Stitched Brute and Bone Rattler, and all six parts, are built from low-poly shapes with a stride animation, a player-coloured sash, and parts that pop on when you gain them and tumble away when you lose them. Monsters are also 40% bigger so they read better on a phone.
- **Five routes:** Osu-Labone Twist, Osu Back Streets, Osu to Cantonments, Jamestown Maze and Ring Road Sprint. The host picks the route from a scrolling list with a small map of each one. Quick match races on a route of the day that changes every day.
- **A new lobby:** all players stand together in a stitching lab. Drag to turn your monster, watch parts pop on as you pick them, see name tags over everyone, and spot who is ready by the green ring. Pick the body and parts from a tabbed grid with a picture of every option.
- **Get up:** if your monster falls on its side, a Get up button appears (Space on a computer). You lose control for two seconds while it stands back up.
- **Share:** send a link to your room from the lobby. A friend who opens it is asked whether to join your race. After a race, share your result.
- **Fixes:** toes no longer sink into the ground.
