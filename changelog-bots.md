## Bots online, skill levels and route changes

- **Bots in online lobbies:** the host can add AI racers to fill empty seats, or send them home again. In quick match, if nobody joins for a while, a notice counts down to bots joining, and you can choose to wait for players instead.
- **Bot skill:** pick Easy, Normal or Hard bots when you practise or host. Bots now swing on poles and lamp posts with their Tentacles too.
- **Players take their seat back:** if someone joins a seat held by a bot, they get it back.
- **Change route:** the host can pick a new route from inside the lobby without making a new room.
- **Idle lobbies close:** if nobody touches the game for five minutes you get a "Still there?" warning, then you are returned to the title screen.
