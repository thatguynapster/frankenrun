## Crab Claws grab, smoother Spider Legs and fairer crashes

- **Crab Claws can grab:** press the arms button with a rival close ahead (within about 6 m) and the claws clamp on and hold. The two of you are tethered, and if the rival does not break free in time, you steal one of their parts. With nobody in reach, the claws stretch out up to 9 m ahead to pull in a loose part. Ramming a rival from behind still steals a part too.
- **Breaking free:** if you are grabbed, swing the steering hard from side to side a few times to break loose. You cannot be grabbed again for a couple of seconds afterwards.
- **Fairer crashes:** a gentle bump no longer counts as a crash. Only a real impact does, and a very hard one counts double, so parts only fall off when you really hit something.
- **Spider Legs walk properly:** each leg now steps, with the foot lifting, swinging forward and planting while your body moves over it, and the knees bend to match.
- **Fix:** grabbing a rival from very close range no longer makes you lose parts as well. Only the rival can lose one.
