## Better touch controls, flickering fixed, and a game that copes with slow connections

- **Slow down and turn around on a phone:** pull your thumb down on the steering drag to ease off the throttle, and pull further to brake. Once you are stopped, steering turns the monster on the spot, so getting out of a dead end is easy. Let go and you are back at full speed.
- **Fixed flickering surfaces:** roads, kerbs and pavements no longer flicker into each other, and buildings that overlap on the map no longer flicker on their roofs and walls.
- **Built for slow and unstable connections:**
  - Joining a race now downloads only the route you are racing on, instead of every route, and a download that drops part way is tried again.
  - The game waits longer for a slow player's controls instead of ignoring them, repeats recent controls so lost data matters less, and adds a little delay to your own controls when your connection needs it, so the race stays in step.
  - Sounds that fail to download are tried again instead of staying silent.
