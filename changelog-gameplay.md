## Minimap, boost pads and two new parts

- **Minimap:** a round map in the top right shows the streets around you, the next checkpoint as a gold ring, and your rivals as dots. It turns with your monster.
- **Boost pads:** glowing pads on the road give you a burst of speed.
- **Oil patches:** dark puddles that make the road slippery, so steer round them.
- **Loose parts on the road:** a part now appears at fixed spots along every route every so often. Drive through one to fill an empty slot.
- **New part, Hopper Haunches:** hold the Leap button (L on keyboard) to crouch, let go to jump over cars and obstacles. You cannot steer in the air, and they are slower than Werewolf Legs on the straights.
- **New part, Hunchback Hump:** keeps a spare part you cannot fit yet, and fits it the moment a slot empties.
