## London joins the race, and the monsters and streets get a proper makeover

- **A new city, London:** pick a city first, then a route. Accra keeps its five routes and London brings three: Westminster to Soho, Shoreditch Back Streets (short, twisty and busy) and Piccadilly to Holborn (wide, straight and fast). London has its own look: brick and cream terraces under slate, plenty of chimneys, and a pea-soup fog. The host's route picker and the in-room route change use the same city-then-route steps, and quick match's route of the day now rotates through every route.
- **Finer monsters:** the Stitched Brute, Bone Rattler and all their parts are rebuilt from smoother, more detailed shapes, with painted colours and a soft rim of moonlight. Tentacles now sit in proper shoulder sockets, and the Rattler's arms are sized to its frame.
- **Limbs stay clear of each other:** arms and legs now push apart when they swing, so they no longer pass through each other or through the body.
- **A real flame:** the Jack-o'-Lantern's fire is now a licking, flickering flame with floating embers.
- **Richer buildings:** windows now have trim, sills and shutters, and some show someone standing in the light. Walls stand on a stone base, taller buildings get ledges, parapets and balconies, houses get overhanging roofs and chimneys, and flat roofs carry water tanks, stairwells and air-conditioning boxes. Shops get a door and a striped awning under their glowing sign.
- **Street level:** raised pavements with kerbs run along the roads and stop at side streets, wide roads get a dashed centre line, parked cars have tapered cabins, wheel caps and tail lights, and there are palm trees and fuller trees. Street lamps are slimmer.
- **Smoother running:** buildings and scenery beyond the fog are no longer drawn, and the city is drawn in fewer steps. Dense maps no longer slow the game down: London's garden walls were the main cause and are now trimmed to the long ones near the route, and the game keeps fewer collision shapes loaded around each monster.
- **Performance readout:** with the info setting on, the readout now breaks down where the time goes (physics, scene, drawing, minimap and graphics card) and counts slow frames.
