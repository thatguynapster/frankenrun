## A Monster Mash makeover, new controls and sound options

- **New look everywhere:** every screen now wears the Monster Mash style: plum-black night panels, pumpkin orange buttons and titles, bone-white text, stitched seams and chunky buttons that press down. The lobby stage is recoloured to match.
- **New logo:** FRANKEN stitched to RUN, with neck bolts and a monster eye that looks around and blinks.
- **New thumbnail:** the store picture is redrawn from the game's own monsters, with the new logo, an orange moon and a stitched border.
- **Menu scenes:** the title screen shows your own monster, in your saved style, turning on the stage. Picking a route shows a slow flyover of that route. Leaving a race no longer leaves the old scene behind the menu.
- **Menu from the title:** a Menu button at the top right of the title screen opens the same settings you get in a race.
- **Pumpkin loading screens:** a glowing jack-o'-lantern with ghost lights circling it shows while connecting and loading.
- **Ability buttons:** bigger, themed buttons with part icons, cooldown rings, and a charge ring for the Leap.
- **Comfort options:** turn camera shake and the minimap on or off, switch the touch controls to left-handed, and set how far you drag to steer.
- **Sound:** separate Master, Effects and Music sliders. The music now loops without a gap. Tentacle ropes make a thud when they hit.
