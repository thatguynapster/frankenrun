## New York joins the race, and the streets, buildings and results get a big upgrade

- **A third city, New York:** four routes through the city that never sleeps. Midtown Grid runs down Broadway between the towers, Lower Manhattan Maze twists from the Battery through the crooked old streets to Chinatown, Brooklyn Brownstones is a long run from Park Slope to DUMBO, and West Village Twist is short and tight where the grid breaks. New York has real-height towers, brick and brownstone, rooftop water towers and a neon blackout night.
- **Two landmarks:** the Elizabeth Tower (Big Ben) stands over the start of Westminster to Soho, with glowing clock faces you can spot through the fog. The Empire State Building rises beside the Midtown Grid, with stepped tiers and a floodlit orange crown.
- **Proper streets:** roads are now continuous surfaces with rounded junctions instead of separate rectangles, so bends and corners join up cleanly. Kerbs and paving-slab pavements curve round each corner, the asphalt is worn and mottled, and there are dashed centre lines, edge lines and zebra crossings. Street lamps light the road and pavement properly, and slime puddles, oil patches and boost pads show clearly on top of the road.
- **Livelier buildings:** every window is now lit separately, so no two buildings repeat a pattern. Shops get glowing display windows on the ground floor, and tall towers get glass walls with lit offices.
- **A live race position:** your place in the race (for example 2 of 4) now updates all along the street, not only when someone reaches a checkpoint.
- **Results that wait for everyone:** when you finish, the race no longer stops for the others. Racers still on the road are shown as "still running" with their checkpoint, and they join the board with their time as they finish. Anyone who loses the connection shows as "left the race", and if you are still racing when the results come up, your game keeps going.
- **Smoother running:** on busy city maps the game was spending a lot of time checking posts and walls that were nowhere near the route. That is gone, so London and New York run much lighter.
