## Monster models, five routes, a new lobby and a living city

- **Real monster models:** your monster now stands on two legs. The Stitched Brute and Bone Rattler, and all six parts, are built from low-poly shapes with a stride animation, a player-coloured sash, and parts that pop on when you gain them and tumble away when you lose them. Monsters are 40% bigger so they read better on a phone.
- **Hits you can see:** monsters squash when they crash, claws snap when a part is stolen, and there are sparks, bursts and a camera shake for crashes, lost parts, steals, pickups, resets and finishing.
- **Five routes:** Osu-Labone Twist, Osu Back Streets, Osu to Cantonments, Jamestown Maze and Ring Road Sprint. The host picks the route from a scrolling list with a small map of each one. Quick match races on a route of the day that changes every day.
- **A new lobby:** all players stand together in a stitching lab. Drag to turn your monster, watch parts pop on as you pick them, see name tags over everyone, and spot who is ready by the green ring. Pick the body and parts from a tabbed grid with a picture of every option.
- **A better-looking city:** buildings now have painted walls, windows (some lit) and roofs. Street lamps line the wider roads with a soft pool of light, and stand well clear of buildings. Buildings that sat in the middle of a road are gone.
- **Lamp posts are useful:** posts beside the straighter parts of the route are solid, so Tentacles can swing round them.
- **Camera cut-away:** when a building gets between the camera and your monster, it dissolves so you can always see where you are going.
- **Slime reworked:** the Slime Gland now spits a blob out of the monster's head. It lands behind you (K, or tap the button) or ahead of you (I, or swipe the button up) and leaves a slippery puddle for rivals. Your own slime never slows you.
- **Get up:** if your monster falls on its side, a Get up button appears (Space on a computer). You lose control for two seconds while it stands back up.
- **Share:** send a link to your room from the lobby. A friend who opens it is asked whether to join your race. After a race, share your result.
- **Fixes:** toes no longer sink into the ground.
