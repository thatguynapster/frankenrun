## Surprises on the road: mystery boxes, street events and wandering creatures

- **Mystery boxes:** stitched mimic crates sit on the road, with glowing eyes, a chomping lid and a floating question mark. Drive through one for a random prize: a speed surge, a shield that stops parts being knocked off, a slime trail that makes rivals behind you slip, a missing part put back, or a jinx that slows you for a moment. Players further back get better prizes, and the leader is a little more likely to be jinxed. A box comes back after 12 seconds.
- **Street events:** every so often something happens on the road just ahead of the leader, with a warning ring and a banner a couple of seconds before. Lightning strikes and knocks monsters about, a slime flood turns a stretch of road slippery, or parts rain down for anyone who needs them. Everyone in the race sees the same event at the same time.
- **Wandering creatures:** a shambling zombie, a sheet ghost and a pumpkin walker stroll back and forth across the road. Bump into one and you get shoved aside and slowed down, so watch the pavements. They wander differently every race.
- **Turning on the spot now matches driving:** when your monster is stopped, A (or steering left) turns it left and D (or steering right) turns it right, the same as in the race. It used to turn the other way.
