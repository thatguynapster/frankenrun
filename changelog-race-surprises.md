## Creatures you can't ignore, and surprises all over the map

- **Mystery boxes everywhere:** stitched mimic crates now sit along every road of the city, not just the race route. Drive through one for a random prize: a speed surge, a shield that stops parts being knocked off (and blocks the creature effects below), a slime trail that makes rivals behind you slip, a missing part put back, or a jinx that slows you for a moment. Players further back get better prizes, and the leader is a little more likely to be jinxed. A box comes back after 12 seconds.
- **Street events:** every so often something happens on the road just ahead of the leader, with a warning ring and a banner a couple of seconds before. Lightning strikes and knocks monsters about, a slime flood turns a stretch of road slippery, or parts rain down for anyone who needs them. Everyone sees the same event at the same time.
- **Creatures all over the map:** zombies, ghosts and pumpkin walkers now roam every street, and each one wanders around its own patch of road instead of pacing a straight line. They look different every race.
  - **Zombies are solid:** you can't drive through one. Hit one hard and you're knocked back and slowed.
  - **Ghosts drift through everything:** a cold aura around them slows you down, and touching one spooks you, reversing your steering for about a second. A "Spooked!" banner tells you what hit you.
  - **Pumpkin walkers are bouncy bumpers:** hit one and you're thrown back. Every few seconds they squash down, with an orange ring showing the reach, then stomp, shoving and slowing everyone close by.
- **New look for creatures and crates:** rebuilt to match the monsters, with rounded stitched bodies, proper boots, a crate lid with interlocking teeth, and a faint aura ring under ghosts.
- **Turning on the spot now matches actual movement:** when your monster is stopped, A (or steering left) turns it left and D (or steering right) turns it right, the same as in the race.
