## AI racers, spooky routes and monster styles

- **Race against bots:** practice races now put you against three AI racers (Ghoul, Wraith, Zombie and friends). They follow the route, slow down for bends, get back up when they tip over, use their Crab Claws and Slime, and pick a different build every race. They count in your position and in the results.
- **A different mood on every route:** Osu-Labone Twist is a moonlit night, Osu Back Streets is a haunted mist, Cantonments sits under a blood moon, Jamestown Maze is the witching hour and Ring Road Sprint is a stormy night with lightning.
- **Ghostly checkpoints:** a glowing circle on the road and spirit wisps drifting around every checkpoint.
- **A busier city:** trees in autumn colours, dead trees, gravestones, glowing pumpkins, glowing shop signs and parked cars. The parked cars are solid, so watch the roadsides.
- **Style your monster:** pick a colour tint and a hat in the lobby (top hat, witch hat, crown, devil horns, antlers or a halo). Everyone in the room sees your look, and the lobby tags and results show it too.
- **Fixes:** the Werewolf Legs, Tentacles and Spider Legs no longer show gaps at their joints, hats sit snugly on every head, the Jack-o'-Lantern's flame goes out under a hat, and lobby name tags no longer pile on top of each other.
