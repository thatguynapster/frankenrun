## Tokyo and Mexico City join the race

- **A fourth city, Tokyo:** four routes through the neon city. Shibuya to Shinjuku is the long fast run, a smooth sweep north past Yoyogi Park. Asakusa to Akihabara starts in the old town near Senso-ji, cuts across a long diagonal and finishes on a flat straight. Ginza to Kanda begins in crooked back streets, so take it carefully, then opens into a clean run up the main avenue. Harajuku to Ebisu is the easy one, a gentle winding line down tree-lined back streets. Tokyo has pale concrete towers, flat roofs, signs on nearly every building and a rainy neon night.
- **A fifth city, Mexico City:** four routes through painted streets at a Day of the Dead dusk. Reforma Run is the long fast one, down Paseo de la Reforma past the Angel of Independence. Centro Histórico Maze starts at La Merced market, cuts through the Zócalo and finishes at Plaza Garibaldi on narrow old streets with plenty of sharp turns. Roma and Condesa is the easy one, a winding line down tree-lined streets with gentle corners. Coyoacán Cobbles is short and twisty, through the colonial lanes around the old square. Mexico City has bright painted facades, low roofs, palm trees and a purple dusk sky.
- **Palm trees only where it is warm:** palms now grow in Accra and Mexico City. London, New York and Tokyo get round trees instead.
